About Me

Hi, I'm Dr. Engy Elgamal. I am a dentist with an interest in artificial intelligence and dental imaging. I enjoy learning how AI can be used to improve panoramic dental X-ray analysis and support future dental research.

I am currently learning about AI tools for medical image analysis and dental research. My goal is to understand how AI can support dentistry and improve panoramic dental X-ray analysis.

Current Project

Panoramic Dental X-ray AI Project

I am working on a project that compares different AI models trained on panoramic dental X-rays.

The goal is to compare the models, evaluate their performance, identify weak classes, and suggest improvements for future versions.

As part of this project, I also built an AI agent that reads evaluation reports and provides a clear comparison between different model versions.
